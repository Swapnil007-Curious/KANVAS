\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\emph{KANs and additive models.} Kolmogorov-Arnold networks place a learnable univariate function, a B-spline with a SiLU base term~\cite{elfwing2018silu}, on every edge~\cite{liu2025kan}, following the Kolmogorov-Arnold representation theorem~\cite{kolmogorov1963representation}; at matched size, MLPs usually match or beat them outside symbolic regression~\cite{yu2024kanormlp}. A one-layer KAN with one edge per input is a generalized additive model~\cite{hastie1986gam} with B-spline shapes~\cite{deboor1978splines}, and a second-difference penalty makes it a P-spline~\cite{eilers1996psplines}. Boosted additive models~\cite{lou2012intelligible}, their pairwise extension~\cite{lou2013ga2m} and the EBM of InterpretML~\cite{nori2019interpretml} are the closest peers; neural additive models fit one network per feature~\cite{agarwal2021nam}. Caruana et al.\ found with an additive model that pneumonia patients with asthma appeared to be at lower risk, a true pattern of the data caused by their care pathway~\cite{caruana2015intelligible}, and Rudin argues for interpretable models in consequential decisions~\cite{rudin2019stop}.

\emph{Explanation, shortcuts and leakage.} LIME~\cite{ribeiro2016lime} and SHAP~\cite{lundberg2017shap} explain any model after the fact; for an additive model the centred shape functions are the exact interventional SHAP values. A shortcut is a rule that works on the standard test distribution and fails on the intended one~\cite{geirhos2020shortcut}. Leakage overstates results~\cite{kaufman2012leakage}, including leakage through preprocessing fitted before the split and through dependent training and test samples, which Kapoor and Narayanan document across many fields~\cite{kapoor2023leakage}; grouped cross-validation is the remedy for grouped data~\cite{roberts2017cv}.

\emph{Failures on GPU clusters.} The trace comes from a workload study of Alibaba PAI~\cite{weng2022mlaas}. On Microsoft's Philly cluster many jobs fail, often from user errors~\cite{jeon2019philly}, and the root causes of deep learning job failures have been classified~\cite{zhang2020dlfailures}. Job terminations have been predicted from job and resource features~\cite{elsayed2017failure}, and history-based predictions support scheduling~\cite{hu2021helios}. Gradient-boosted trees such as LightGBM~\cite{ke2017lightgbm} remain strong on tabular data~\cite{grinsztajn2022tree}. We know of no failure-prediction study on these traces that separates known from new users.
