\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On the full Alibaba PAI 2020 trace, the models with interactions lead on random splits, where many test tasks duplicate a training task; with users held out no model is clearly best, and every model loses much of its accuracy on new users and in the final week of the trace. The readable model's curves and exact contributions located one shortcut, a one-core rule learned from one user. Failure predictors for shared clusters should be evaluated with grouped and time-ordered protocols that report known and new users separately and check for duplicated tasks.
