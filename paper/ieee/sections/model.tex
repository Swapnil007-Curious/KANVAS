\section{Model}
\label{sec:model}

\kaam{} predicts $p(\text{fail}\mid x)=\sigma\big(b+\sum_{i=1}^{d}\phi_i(x_i)\big)$ with $\phi_i(x)=w_i\,\mathrm{SiLU}(x)+\sum_j c_{ij}B_j(x)$, the KAN edge form~\cite{liu2025kan} applied once per feature: cubic B-splines on \V{model.knots} interior knots, \V{model.params} parameters in total. Each edge sits in its own slot of a PyTorch \texttt{ModuleDict} and sees only its own column, so additivity is structural; the cost is that \kaam{} has no interactions. The model is a P-spline generalized additive model fitted by gradient descent, and nothing in the audit below needs more than additivity. The loss adds $\lambda\sum_{i,j}(c_{i,j+1}-2c_{i,j}+c_{i,j-1})^2$ to the cross-entropy~\cite{eilers1996psplines}, with $\lambda\in$\V{model.lambdas} chosen on validation. Training uses Adam~\cite{kingma2015adam} (learning rate \V{model.lr}, minibatches of \V{model.batch}, at most \V{model.maxepochs} epochs) and stops after \V{model.patience} epochs without a gain in validation ROC-AUC, restoring the best epoch. Centred on the mean training task, each $\phi_i(x_i)$ is the exact SHAP value of feature $i$~\cite{lundberg2017shap}, as for any additive model.

\emph{Baselines.} All models see the same inputs. Logistic regression (LR; L2, $C$ over \V{model.lr.ngrid} values from \V{model.lr.cmin} to \V{model.lr.cmax}) is the linear additive floor. A ReLU MLP with hidden layers \V{model.mlp.shapes} (\V{model.mlp.params} parameters; learning rate \V{model.mlp.lrs}) is the parameter-matched black box. LightGBM~\cite{ke2017lightgbm} (learning rate \V{model.lgbm.lr}; \V{model.lgbm.leaves} leaves crossed with minimum leaf size \V{model.lgbm.minleaf}; early stopping on validation AUC) is the strong tabular baseline. The EBM~\cite{nori2019interpretml} enters as the additive peer (\texttt{interactions=0}, EBM-A) and with its default pairwise terms (EBM-P), at the package defaults; unlike the other models, the EBMs stop early on an internal random holdout of their training split. Stochastic models use \V{prot.seeds} seeds per split. Under the protocols the EBMs are fitted on the original sample only, since one fit on a full training split takes hours of CPU time; one additive EBM was fitted on the full trace's canonical split (random split \V{prot.canon}, seed~0) for the shape comparison.
